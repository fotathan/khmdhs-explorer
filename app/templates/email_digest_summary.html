{# email_digest_summary.html — the SUMMARY body of a scheduled results digest. The other shape of the same email (see email_digest.html, which lists the acts). This one answers "how much, of what, from whom" and sends the reader into the app for the detail: for a profile that matches a hundred acts a day, a list nobody scrolls is a worse message than four numbers and a button. NOT a page: rendered by app/digests.py through its own Jinja environment (no request, no base template, no context processor) because the same render has to work from cron. The only context is what render_digest passes in, `t` included, and every figure arrives already formatted by _summary_view — the text/plain alternative prints the same strings, so the two cannot drift. Mail-client rules, not browser rules: tables for layout, inline styles only (Gmail strips
